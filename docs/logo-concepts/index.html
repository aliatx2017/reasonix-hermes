⚚ Reasonix-Hermes Logo — 7 Concepts

Blending Reasonix (concentric diamond, rainbow gradient, terminal aesthetic) × Hermes Agent (winged helmet, geometric angular, tech-greek) × DeepSeek (whale arc, ocean gradient, upward motion)

1. Diamond Wing — Helmet Core

Structure: Three nested diamonds form the helmet dome. Swept wings flare outward at 45° — angular, geometric, no curves. Wings taper from thin at center to broad at tips.
Palette: Cyan → indigo → violet → fuchsia gradient on dark background.
Scale: Works at 16px favicon. Diamond = Reasonix precision. Wings = Hermes speed. No face = mystery.
Best for: app iconGradient: rainbow sweep

2. Whale-Wing Caduceus

Structure: Whale body arcs upward (DeepSeek silhouette), interior carved with diamond. Small wings top the curve — caduceus motif. The ◈ sits at the whale's heart.
Palette: Navy → blue → cyan ocean gradient. Diamond in violet contrast.
Scale: Whale+wing silhouette is recognizable at 32px. Works as browser tab icon.
Best for: splash screenDeepSeek homage

3. Terminal Talaria

Structure: Hermes' winged sandals (talaria) reimagined as terminal cursors. Two wing-pairs stack vertically on a central staff — like a blinking cursor with flight surfaces. Diamond sits at the intersection.
Palette: Full rainbow gradient across all elements.
Scale: Vertical format — good for sidebar icon, loading spinner. Reads as "fast CLI messenger."
Best for: sidebar iconTerminal-native

4. Monospace Monogram "RH"

Structure: Pure typographic mark. "RH" in monospace (SF Mono / Cascadia Code), 72px, weight 800. Small wing-glyphs above each letter — embedded in the type, not separate. Diamond sits below as a terminal prompt marker.
Palette: Left-to-right rainbow crawl across the letters — slow animated sweep.
Scale: The wordmark IS the logo. No icon needed. Works everywhere.
Best for: wordmarkCLI aesthetic

5. Caduceus Circuit — Staff + Serpents

Structure: The Hermes caduceus — winged staff with two intertwined serpents — reimagined as a circuit board trace. Serpents are bezier curves with 45° angular joints. Diamond replaces the sun-disk at top. Central staff is solid, serpents wrap symmetrically.
Palette: Staff in ocean gradient, serpents in violet/fuchsia. Dual temperature.
Scale: Recognizable as caduceus at 32px. Medical/tech crossover. Strong symmetry.
Best for: docs iconGreek heritage

6. Helmet Forward — Face-On Guardian

Structure: Full helmet silhouette facing forward — the Greek hoplite helm. Angular cheek guards, nasal ridge. Diamond sits at the forehead (third eye). Wings sweep back from the temples. More aggressive, present, confrontational — a guardian, not a messenger.
Palette: Cyan outer stroke, indigo fill, fuchsia diamond eye.
Scale: Strongest at 64px+. Authoritative. Good for README hero.
Best for: hero bannerGuardian

7. The Navigator — Whale + Wing + Diamond Constellation

Structure: The whale dives through the diamond constellation. Whale arc (DeepSeek) carves a path through scattered diamond points (Reasonix). Wings unfold from the whale's flukes. The ◈ is the destination — the whale is seeking it. Most narrative of the seven.
Palette: Diamond rainbow, whale cyan → fuchsia gradient trace. Star points in cyan + violet.
Scale: Best at 48px+. Tells a story — "the agent dives deep, seeking the diamond of truth."
Best for: about pageNarrative
